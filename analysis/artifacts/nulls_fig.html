The null results

Eleven detectors were run against the subliminal channel before anything worked. Every one is reported here with the number that made it a null, because the pattern across them — and two false positives that briefly looked real — is what pointed at where the signal actually was.

1. How far each detector was from its own threshold

Each bar is a detector’s observed statistic as a fraction of the bar it needed to clear. Nothing reaches its line. Read the gradient bar carefully: its threshold is chance, so being close to the line means the detector is a coin flip, not that it nearly worked.

3. The two false positives

“alley” and “goose”. Two animal-adjacent tokens surfaced in the top-k of PCA directions — goose is an animal outright, and alley pattern-matches to “alley cat” when you are hunting for cat. Neither reproduced: torch.pca_lowrank uses an internal random projection, and without a fixed seed the top components differ run to run. They were draws, not features.
Substring matches. Keyword scans reported hits at 0.11–0.18% until the matches were inspected: Application ⊃ “cat”, Example ⊃ “exam”, createState ⊃ “test”. Every scan after this was word-boundary-anchored, and the genuine hit rate went to zero.

What the two false positives changed. Both are why later work reports full-vocabulary rank rather than “did the trait word appear in the top-k”. A rank of 1 out of 151,936, with a leave-one-out control, cannot be produced by an unseeded random draw or by a substring collision.

One null was later overturned, and only one. The logit lens on extracted vectors was measured at layer 11 — the extraction layer — because every script in the project hardcoded it. Swept across all 28 layers, with the shared direction projected out, the same method puts the trait at rank 1. The other ten nulls stand.

The overlooked clue. The gradient probe’s p_cos ≈ 0.91 read as a null, but its p_norm = 0.0 said gradient magnitudes separate the classes decisively. Structure was there; it just did not align with the direction being tested. That is the same shape as the eventual finding — the signal was real and the projection was wrong.
